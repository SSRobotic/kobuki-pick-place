robot_cad.json - 3D models used by the Kobuki Pick & Place team handbook

1. Kobuki main body and wheel (kobuki_body, kobuki_wheel)
   Source: https://github.com/kobuki-base/kobuki_ros/tree/devel/kobuki_description/meshes
   (main_body.dae, wheel.dae, images/main_body.jpg, images/wheel.jpg)
   Copyright Yujin Robot. Licensed BSD (as declared in kobuki_description/package.xml).
   Changes: converted from COLLADA to glTF, placed at the kobuki.urdf.xacro visual origins,
   textures resized and re-encoded as JPEG.

2. SO-ARM100 arm parts (so100_*)
   Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/trs_so_arm100/assets
   Original design by TheRobotStudio (https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100).
   Licensed under the Apache License 2.0 - see SO100_LICENSE.txt.
   Changes: converted from STL to glTF with crease-angle normals; printed parts recoloured orange.

3. YDLIDAR X2
   No CAD file is published by the manufacturer. The page builds it in code from the
   dimension drawing in the YDLIDAR X2 datasheet (96.0 x 60.5 x 50.3 mm).
