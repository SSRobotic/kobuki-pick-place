ydlidar_x2_base.stl, ydlidar_x2_top.stl
  Made for this project from the dimension drawing in the YDLIDAR X2 datasheet
  (96.0 x 60.5 x 50.3 mm); YDLIDAR publishes no CAD file for the X2. Base = bracket + motor,
  top = the spinning cap (navy on the real unit). Origin = laser centre.

xtion.stl
  ASUS Xtion PRO LIVE, made for this project from its published size (body 180 x 35 x 38 mm, on its stand) and photos.

deck_plate.stl
  The team's acrylic deck plates (300 x 280 x 5 mm, round corners), sized from photos of the robot.

All three are generated by tools/make_meshes.py (run it again after changing a size).
